What you paste

One tag, anywhere in your page

<script src="https://app.quotecraft.io/embed.js" data-quotecraft="your-slug"></script>

Drop it where you want the calculator to appear. It loads in its own frame and resizes itself to fit, so your theme's CSS can't break it and its CSS can't break your theme.

What happens after

Every visitor who reaches a price is asked for their name and contact details. That becomes a lead in your dashboard with the full quote attached — the size they picked, the service, the add-ons, the total.

Add a webhook URL and the same lead is pushed to Zapier, Make, HighLevel or your own endpoint within seconds, so it can start an automation before they've closed the tab.

Prices are recalculated on our server before anything is saved, so nobody can fiddle with the page and book a $2,000 job for $9.
