How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

You set your prices once. After that, your website quotes every visitor the same way you would — without you touching the phone.

STEP 01

Set your numbers

Start from a preset built for your trade, then set your hourly rate, minimum charge, recurring discounts and add-ons. Everything is priced off crew time, so the maths always matches how you actually work.

STEP 02

Paste one line

Drop a single script tag into your site — Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, HighLevel, anything. The calculator loads in its own frame, so nothing about your theme can break it.

STEP 03

Collect the leads

Every customer who sees a price gets asked for their details. Those land in your dashboard and get pushed to your CRM through Zapier, Make or any webhook.
